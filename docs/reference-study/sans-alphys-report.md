# Storyboard review: Sans (Genocide) vs fan-made Alphys fight

Sources and method
- Video A: "[Undertale] Sans' boss fight - Genocide Run" (Suwako Moriya), 888 s, 179 storyboard frames, about 4.96 s apart (sheets 00-19).
- Video B: "Undertale Alphys fight" (Cupcake Bruh [Archive]), 661 s, 131 frames, about 5.05 s apart (sheets 00-14; the last tiles are padding or repeated load screens).
- Every sheet was viewed at 2x, and key tiles were cropped at 4x. Times are frame index x interval, so each one is accurate to about +/-5 s. A storyboard frame is one instant in a 5 s window. Attack durations are therefore estimates (number of frames an attack is visible x about 5 s), and short attacks can fall between frames and not appear at all.
- Labels: **[frames]** means visible in the storyboard. **[wiki]** means stated in the Undertale wiki text (`scratchpad/ref/f-sans-battle.txt`). **[inference]** is my interpretation. Video B is a fan game, so the wiki does not cover it.
- HP numbers are read from low-resolution tiles, so treat single digits as +/-1 or 2.

### A3. Turn structure and escalation (Sans)
- **Sans attacks first** [frames + wiki].
- **Phase 1 (≈25-220 s), blue mode.** The player turn plus dialogue plus attack repeats about every **15 s** (MISS frames at 25, 65, 80, 95, 110, 125, 140, 155, 170 s). Dialogue appears in short bubbles, often *during* the attack frame, so it doesn't add dead time. The attack order follows the wiki list: bone lines, blue bones, randomized lines, Platforms 1-4, then platforms with Blasters. [frames + wiki]
- **False mercy (≈205-215 s).** "Sans is sparing you." This is a safe heal window [wiki]. The player refills to 92.
- **Phase 2 (≈220-445 s).** Starts with a black flicker frame and displaced sprite and box. The SOUL alternates red and blue. Square slam boxes and Blaster volleys appear, and bones attack the menu. Turns lengthen to about **20 s** (≈10 turns in 225 s), because of menu bones, item use and flavor text. The flavor text escalates: "You felt your sins..." → "Reading this doesn't seem..." → "Sans is preparing something". [frames + wiki]
- **Finale.** A ≈45 s final attack ends at HP 1. Then about 100 s of monologue and about 200 s of idle stall ("special attack"). The player must wait for sleep, then drag the box onto FIGHT. One hit kills Sans. The death scene runs ≈40 s, then LV 20.
- **Time budget.** Combat ≈15-490 s (≈475 s, about 8 min). Non-combat finale ≈495-833 s (≈5.6 min). Ending ≈833-888 s.

## B4. What makes the fan Alphys fight distinct
1. **Machinery instead of magic.** Lasers come from visible hardware: drones, pods, pylons, dome turrets and wire-mounted bombs. Alphys triggers them with a **remote**. Sans's blasters are "magic" skulls. [frames]
2. **Attacking is self-damaging ("SHOCKED").** The fight punishes FIGHT with about 7-11 HP of damage, and no Alphys HP is visible. [frames]
3. **SOUL modes.** Only a **red** SOUL was seen in phase 1. No blue gravity mode, platforms or yellow shooter mode appeared. **Cyan/orange laser colours** are the colour-coded mechanic instead. In phase 2 the **SOUL sprite itself changes** (cracked or outlined, then white). [frames; cyan/orange meaning is base-game convention]
4. **Box geometry as the main escalation tool.** Beveled, rotated-hexagon, octagon, notched, parallelogram and 45-degree diamond boxes. Sans only changes the box's *size* (square ↔ wide ↔ screen-wide) and *position*. [frames]
5. **Allies on the enemy side, not the player's.** The Amalgamate-like figures merge into Alphys's sprite in phase 2 and spawn their own bullets. No ally of the player appears. [frames]
6. **UI corruption.** Creatures occupy the menu buttons one by one and turn them white. This is a different idea from Sans's menu *bones*, which deal damage. [frames + wiki]
7. **Telegraphs** are explicit and "technical": dotted crosshairs, a reticle and a dashed red diagonal. Sans uses red slam lines and blaster wind-up sprites. [frames]
8. **Ending** is a narrative cutscene ("Surrender.") rather than a mechanical trick like Sans's sleep-and-drag. [frames]

## B5. Pacing and readability observations for a designer
1. **Dead air vs. dense air.** Sans keeps bullets on screen for most of each 15 s cycle and puts his lines in bubbles *during* attacks. The Alphys fight spends about 2-4 frames (10-20 s) per turn on stuttered dialogue in an empty box. For a combined "Sans + Alphys" fight, consider letting one character talk while the other attacks.
2. **Teach, then remix.** Sans's phase 1 introduces one idea per turn (lines → blue bone → platforms 1-4 → platforms + blaster). Phase 2 recombines them with slams and blasters. The Alphys fight introduces a new gadget almost every turn and rarely repeats one. That reads as variety, but there is less chance to master a pattern.
3. **Colour language.** Sans relies on white/light-blue bones and red/blue SOUL. Alphys's cyan/orange beams map onto the same base-game rules, so they read clearly to Undertale players. Mixing both casts means **light-blue bones and cyan lasers would share one rule**, which is good for consistency. Avoid using the same yellow for lasers and for UI highlights (both appear in B).
4. **Telegraph clarity.** The clearest telegraphs in the frames are the red slam lines (A), the dotted crosshair plus reticle (B) and the dashed red diagonal (B). Lightning grids (B, 287 s) and the blaster ring (A, 465 s) cover the whole screen and **hide the HP bar and menu**. That is fine for climaxes, but they are hard to read.
5. **Box shape as a difficulty dial.** B's rotated and beveled boxes are visually distinctive, but SOUL position becomes harder to read against diagonal edges. Sans keeps the box axis-aligned and changes only size and position (and the full-screen corridor for the finale).
6. **Menu interaction.** Sans's menu bones (damage but cannot kill) and B's button takeover are two different "out of control" ideas. Both hold attention during the player's turn.
7. **Healing rhythm.** In A, the player heals during the safe false-mercy turn and at a few menu turns. In B the player eats on about 5 of ≈14 turns, partly because FIGHT self-damages. If the combined fight keeps "SHOCKED", budget healing for it.
8. **Long non-combat finales.** Sans's ≈5.6 min of monologue and idle stall is iconic but slow (the video player triggered about 6 eye-flash resets). B's ≈65 s narration is shorter. Pick one finale style, or make one skippable on retries.
9. **Sampling caveat.** Five-second frames under-sample fast patterns (Sans's opening ≈10 s and multi-stage final attack especially). Verify exact durations against the source videos before copying any timing.
