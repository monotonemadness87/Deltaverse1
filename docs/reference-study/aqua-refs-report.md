### 3. Rival-player / "human" elements

- The enemy is a human (a Chara analogue) with a bloody knife. All bullets are knives or slash lines, not magic projectiles, which fits a knife-fighter.
- **Pre-fight monologue as a rival**: "partner", "Let's enjoy ourselves on this new experiment", "I'll show you something new". She treats the fight as a game between two players who know about resets.
- **Expression changes**: she smiles, looks shocked or wide-eyed, and flashes white when hit. A red damage number appears beside her face after FIGHT.
- **Flowey on the sword pedestal** acts as a spectator. He fills up with hearts as the fight goes on, so he works like a progress meter.
- **The player** heals with ITEM at least twice when HP is low (i51→i52, i65→i66).
- No enemy dodge, parry or item use was seen. There are no custom gauges (no TP/KR bar). Apart from the attack bar, the UI is standard Undertale.
- Cinematic moments: Flowey's execution, the red void monologue with a glitch flash, and the **finale that strips the entire screen down to the box**.

### 4. Turn structure, escalation, ending

- The loop is: menu (FIGHT or ACT) → short enemy reaction → attack (≈5–15 s) → menu. The player alternates FIGHT (5 or more attack-bar frames) with ACT (3 clothing stages, i33, i43, i58). Each ACT stage changes the sprite and Flowey's reaction. The ACT stages appear to work as the phase triggers.
- Escalation: the line volleys go from a few lines to dense crosses. Knives join the hand sweep, and the star snipe gains pendulum knives. The later phase adds the subscribe caption (an uploader overlay) and denser patterns.
- Ending: a ~25–30 s vortex/knife-ring finale with no background, then "Continue? Yes/No", then a calm overworld scene where the player walks up to Chara on the golden flowers. No defeat or spare animation was captured. It is unclear whether the battle ended in a win, a scripted end, or a death.

### 5. Visual style and pacing

- Palette: black base; dark crimson and pink line-art background; the enemy's saturated green/yellow sweater; a gold fire platform; **white knives**; red and orange telegraph lines. The finale is almost monochrome (grey vortex, white knives, a red band).
- **The weapons are small** next to the very large enemy sprite. Bullet knives are about SOUL-sized to 2× SOUL. The giant red knife-and-hand sprite is the one big weapon visual.
- Screen use: the line attacks use the **whole screen** and run through the background and UI. The box itself stays small and central.
- Readability: telegraph-then-strike lines (red, then white) read well even at storyboard resolution. The grey translucent band and the faint vortex are hard to read at low resolution. The busy background plus the uploader overlays clutter the frame.
- Pacing: the dialogue intro is long (~100 s of monologue). Each attack is short (≈5–15 s). The whole battle is about 230 s (i30 to i75).

- **"BLOCK" parry**: on at least 4 FIGHT turns (i36, i60, i64, i69), Chara holds her knife horizontally and a large "BLOCK" label appears over her head. The player's attack is negated. This is the clearest **rival-player defence** in either video. On other FIGHT turns (i40, i47) she takes or reacts to the slash (raised arm, red crescent).
- **Taunting dialogue about save files and resets** ("I was sure that I loaded my save point after we died…", "Even if I kill you, you've come back as if it never happened…"). She talks like someone who also knows the game's mechanics, like a rival player.
- **Announced special move**: "I guess I have no choice but to use my "Special Attack"." / "So don't die on me just yet, okay?", followed by a new attack type.
- **Pose changes** tied to attacks: knife raised overhead (beam/slash), knife held across the body (BLOCK), eyes closed with flowers, hunched and clutching her chest (defeat).
- **MERCY is removed** partway through (from i83). This signals a phase change and a no-spare fight.
- **Mirrored/greyed screen frames** (i63, i68, i87, i92, i101, i102) appear between attacks. This is a disorientation or flashback effect, possibly tied to the save/load theme.
- **Player item use**: ITEM at i58 (HP ~15 → ~55) and the Butterscotch Pie at i104 (HP maxed). There are also heals between frames (i73→i74, i91→i93). **The enemy was not seen using items** (the flower frames could be an item or an attack; that is unclear).
- **No custom TP/KR gauge** on screen. The UI additions are the red glow gradient, the custom attack bar, the BLOCK label and red-coloured key words in the dialogue.
- Cinematic moments: the FIGHT-only button during the monologue (the player must choose to fight), the final no-UI barrage, the white flash, the split heart, "But nobody came.", and the credits.

### 4. Turn structure, escalation, ending

- The loop is: menu (flavour text "* The smell…" etc.) → FIGHT/ITEM → attack bar → **BLOCK or hit** → speech bubble → attack (~1–3 frames ≈ 10–30 s) → menu. One full cycle takes about 30–60 s. There are 12 or more FIGHT turns over ~780 s of combat (i27–i110).
- Escalation: simple flanking knives → walls and grids → box squeeze and relocation → mirrored-screen flashes → announced "Special Attack" → MERCY removed, orange/cyan fills and the big red slash → dialogue cue "knife burn more and more brighter" → corner X and laser convergence → "a promise is a promise" → final barrage without UI (box only, knife ring).
- Ending: the barrage ends and Chara is hunched and wounded, delivering a closing monologue. The UI text glitches, then a white flash, a tiny split red heart, "But nobody came." repeated, the credits, a broken heart, and the title screen.

### 5. Visual style and pacing

- Palette: almost entirely **black and white line art** (the enemy, box, knives), with **red** reserved for meaning: her knife, key words in dialogue ("KING", "MONSTER", "Special Attack"), the screen-bottom glow, the big slash and the laser lines. **Orange and cyan** mark rule-based knives, and **yellow** marks telegraph grids and flowers.
- Weapons: the knives are clean white outlines at about 1–2× SOUL length, readable even at 240x180. Signature moves are **big**: full-screen white beams, the jagged red slash covering the box, the 24-knife ring.
- Screen use: knives spawn around Chara's body and outside the box, and beams span the full screen. The box moves and resizes. The final attack removes the UI to focus on the box.
- Readability: strong. It uses one colour language (white = hazard, red = danger/emphasis, orange/cyan = rule), telegraph lines before beams, and a big "BLOCK" label so the player instantly knows why no damage landed. The mirrored grey frames are the least readable element.
- Pacing: a long build-up (~265 s before the fight). Inside the fight, dialogue sits between attacks, so attacks are short bursts with breathing room. There is a clear crescendo through the announced special attack, the MERCY removal and the UI-free finale. The fight runs about 13 minutes, plus ~3 minutes of ending and credits.

## Cross-video notes relevant to Aqua (observations, not design decisions)

- Both enemies are knife-wielding humans. Every bullet is a knife, a slash line or a beam. Neither uses magic shapes except Video B's grey hearts and orb.
- Only **Video B** shows a rival-player defence (**BLOCK** parry with a big label) and an **announced special move**. Neither shows the enemy dodging, grazing, using items or having a TP gauge on screen.
- Both use **telegraph-then-strike lines across the full screen**: red, then white in A; red/yellow, then white beams in B.
- Both end with a **finale that strips away the UI and background**, leaving only the box.
- Both mark phases with menu changes: A's ACT stages, and B's MERCY removal plus the red glow.
- Both use **a hidden or offered FIGHT choice before the fight** (B) or a long monologue (A and B).
