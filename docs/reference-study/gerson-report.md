# Storyboard review: "(No Hit) Secret Gerson Fight | Deltarune Ch4" (Kippy, 273 s)

## Source and limits

- There are 16 storyboard sheets. Sheets 00–14 hold 9 frames each. **Sheet 15 holds only 3 frames (960x180)**, so there are **138 frames (#0–#137)**, not 144. Frame time is about i × 1.896 s, so the frames cover about 0–260 s. The last ~13 s of the video (≈260–273 s) has no frames.
- The frames are ~1.9 s apart and about 320x180 each. Short events (individual bullets, the moment a turn changes, telegraph flashes shorter than ~2 s) can fall between frames. **All durations below are approximate (frame count × ~1.9 s)**, and "appears to" marks anything I could not read with confidence.
- The party is **Susie alone**. Her HP reads about **"230/23x"**. The digits are too blurry to read exactly, but the HP bar stays full in every frame (this is a no-hit run). The enemy's name on the target menu is **"Hammer of Justice"**. Its HP bar looks full and green (the % text is not legible), and MERCY shows **"???"** (frame #55, ~104 s).
- The background changes colour from turn to turn: green, then cyan/blue stained glass, then a starry night, then a pink/orange sunset with statues, then magenta. The colour shifts each time an attack or a Rude Buster happens.

## 2. Enemy attack patterns

Counts cover the frames only. A turn can mix patterns.

### A. Shield (green SOUL, directional block)
- **Start:** The square box spins/zooms in, sometimes rotated with a green swirl inside. The SOUL is green and **fixed in the centre**. There is no visible separate telegraph before the bullets.
- **Bullets:** Small **hammers** (pink/red, sometimes white) and, later, small **spinning yellow shells** (green/cyan shells in later turns). They are seen **outside the box first**, above, below, left, right and later diagonal, then travel straight in toward the centre. **Yellow arrow-shaped markers** appear off-box and at the screen edges on the incoming sides, and appear to warn of incoming direction, but that is not confirmed. The player turns a **cyan axe-head shield** (with a red bar showing the facing edge) around the SOUL to block. TP rises steadily during these turns (about +2 to +6 per frame), apparently from blocking.
- **Box:** A normal-size square that stays still. The **inner guard frame is a square (4 directions) in turns 1–3** and **switches to an octagon (8 directions, so diagonals) from mid turn 3 (~36 s) on**.
- **Length:** About 8–10 s on its own (4–5 frames), or 11–13 s with shells (6–7 frames).
- **Appearances:** Main part of **9 turns** (1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13) plus the opening of turn 14, so **10 turns use it**.
- **Escalation:** Square → octagon; hammers only → hammers + yellow shells (from ~119 s) → several shells on screen at once from different sides (~135 s) → mixed green and yellow shells (~205–233 s).

### B. Dive finisher (red SOUL, falling Gerson lanes)
- **Start:** At the end of a shield turn the SOUL **switches green → red** and can now move. A **white Gerson silhouette** shows above or outside the box, and a **light-grey or white lane** fills the stripe he will hit.
- **Movement:** Gerson (or clones) dives **head-first along the lane**. Lanes are vertical at first; in the final turn they become **diagonal**, and the fight ends with a **ring of ~7 clones aimed inward** at the box.
- **Box:** A normal square that shrinks a little by the end of the last turn.
- **Length:** About 2–6 s at the end of a turn (1–3 frames). It is easy to miss between frames.
- **Appearances:** Seen after the shield phase of **turn 4 (~53 s), turn 10 (~152–155 s) and turn 14 (~235–243 s)**. It grows each time: 1 lane → 2 lanes and 2 clones → diagonal lanes and a radial ring.

### C. Shell & stars (red SOUL)
- **Start:** Gerson **glows green** at his spot on the right (#31), then turns into a **spinning shell**.
- **Movement:** A **green turtle shell** moves across the box (left → right along the top edge) and drops a **trail of white stars**. Stars drift across the box from Susie's side and then **rain down over the top of the box**, with grey dust clouds on the floor at both sides. Gerson, shown as a spinning tornado, waits at the right.
- **Box:** A normal square that does not move.
- **Length:** About 13 s (7 frames).
- **Appearances:** **Once** (turn 5, ~59–70 s).

### D. Hammer storm (red SOUL)
- **Start:** The square box **rotates 45° into a diamond**. Gerson jumps into the air on the right.
- **Movement:** A dense stream of **grey/white spinning hammers** thrown in **arcs from the upper right**. They sweep down and left through the diamond and leave the screen at the left and bottom. Gerson moves around the right side while throwing. It ends with a **large hammer** near the box.
- **Box:** A diamond (rotated square). In the repeat it **starts small and grows** into the diamond.
- **Length:** About 13 s (7 frames).
- **Appearances:** **Twice** (turn 6, ~80–91 s; turn 12, ~182–193 s).

### E. Wide-box Gerson dives (red SOUL)
- **Start:** The box **stretches into a long horizontal strip**. Gerson pops up above it.
- **Movement:** **Grey lanes** mark columns, then Gerson clones dive head-down through them. It escalates from single dives, to a **left-to-right sweep of staggered lanes** (white → grey afterimages), to **5–6 clones diving at once**, leaving only the edge of the strip safe.
- **Box:** A wide, short strip about 2.5–3× normal width.
- **Length:** About 13 s (7 frames).
- **Appearances:** **Once** (turn 11, ~167–178 s).

## 3. Turn structure

- **Strict alternation:** one player action, then one enemy turn. There are **14 player actions and 14 enemy turns** in the frames, plus a FIGHT at 4 s before the first enemy turn (which counts as player action 1).
- **Player side:** Susie alternates between **FIGHT** (Gerson **always "MISS"es / dodges**, 7 times) and **RUDE BUSTER** (8 times, including the final "* Susie…" spell). A FIGHT turn is followed by a jump of about +24 TP (38→62, 22→46, 25→49, 26→50, 27→51). The frames do not show why. Rude Buster costs about **40% TP** each time (77→37, 54→14, 52→12, 61→21, 40→0, 58→18, 69→29, 43→4). Player turns are short, about **2–8 s** (1–4 frames), mostly the attack animation.
- **Enemy turns:** About **8–10 s** early (turns 1–4) and about **11–13 s** from turn 5 on. The final turn lasts about **20 s** (#119–129) because it chains a shield section into a long dive sequence.
- **Order of enemy turns:** Shield, Shield, Shield, Shield+dive, **Shell & stars**, **Hammer storm**, Shield, Shield (+shells), Shield (+shells), Shield+dive, **Wide-box dive**, **Hammer storm**, Shield (mixed), **Shield + big dive finale**. The **Shield** turns (green, defensive) are the backbone. The **red-SOUL "spectacle" attacks** (C, D, E) are placed between them and appear to come right after a FIGHT turn, though the frames are too few to confirm that as a rule.
- **Escalation / phases:** There is no clear new phase or health threshold on screen. Gerson's HP looks full when the bar is shown at 104 s, and his attacks are always dodged. Difficulty instead **grows gradually inside each pattern**: the shield frame goes square → octagon, the bullets add yellow and then green shells, and more come at once; the dive finisher goes 1 lane → 2 → diagonal + radial; the box gets more varied (square → diamond → wide strip). Gerson's speech bubble first appears at ~112 s, though its text cannot be seen.
- **Ending:** After the final turn the battle UI stays visible but the menu does not return. Susie says "... Alright. I figured it out. / You wanna see what I got: / Take TH…" and Gerson answers "Ha, ya…". The screen then **fades to white (~256–260 s)**. The fight ends in a scripted cutscene, not by defeating Gerson. The last ~13 s of the video have no frames.

## 4. Pacing and readability notes

- **Constant breathing room:** every enemy turn is followed by a short player turn (~2–8 s), so the player is never under fire for more than ~13 s in a row, except in the ~20 s final turn.
- **Telegraphs:**
  - Dives give **two warnings**: a white Gerson silhouette and a grey/white lane covering the danger zone before the body arrives. The lane is big and high-contrast (grey on black), so it is easy to read even at storyboard resolution. The warning appears at least ~2 s before the dive, since it is caught in single frames before the dive frame.
  - Shield bullets are **visible well outside the box** before they arrive, and the yellow off-screen arrows appear to show the incoming side. Projectiles start far away, which leaves time to turn the shield.
  - The hammer storm and the stars have **no obvious separate telegraph** in the frames. The warning is seeing Gerson jump or glow and the stream arriving from one corner (upper right), which still gives a readable "safe side".
- **Density:** The shield turns stay sparse, with 1–3 projectiles on screen at a time. The **hammer storm is the densest pattern** (20+ hammers on screen in #44–46 and #98–101), but it moves in one arc direction. The **wide-box dive** and the **radial finale** fill most of the safe space at once, leaving one gap (the far end of the strip, or the box centre).
- **Mode switches:** the change from green to red SOUL in the middle of a turn (the dive finisher) is a notable surprise beat, marked by the heart changing colour and the white silhouette. A designer copying it should keep that switch clearly shown.
- **Box choreography as signal:** the box spinning in and out marks the start and end of each turn, and the box shape mostly predicts the attack: diamond means hammer storm, a wide strip means dives, a square with an inner frame means shield.
- **Visual noise:** the background colour changes every turn or two, which keeps things fresh but means bullets (pink hammers, yellow shells) must stand out against several palettes. In the frames they stay readable against the dimmed background during attacks.
- **Reward loop:** blocking in shield turns visibly builds TP, and grazing (white outline seen in red-SOUL turns) does too. That TP is spent on Rude Buster every other turn, so the defensive turns feed the offence.
