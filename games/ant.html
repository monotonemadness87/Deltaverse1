THE FIELD GUIDE

The colony is the story.

01 / Watch a real worker

Tap an insect. Inspect its task and cargo, then press Follow. It can gather outside, enter the nest, deliver to stores, and return to work. Zoom into the cutaways to see nurses and brood.

02 / Change conditions

Choose Protein, Sugar or Dead wood, then tap the forest floor. Stones block routes. Breach opens a nest. Rain changes moisture and movement. Royal removal tests whether a colony can replace its breeders.

03 / Let nature respond

Workers discover nearby food; returning traffic marks trails. Food and care support new adults. Crowding causes excavation. Termites build shelter, and their soldiers depend on workers for food.

04 / Explore both worlds

Woodland colonies live in a log and an underground network. In the savanna, Matabele scouts recruit raids on termite foraging sites; their food comes from prey, while termites cultivate fungus.
